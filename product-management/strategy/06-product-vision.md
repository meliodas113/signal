# Product Vision & Strategy

## Vision

> **Every deep-tech founder should be able to find, judge, and never miss the non-dilutive federal
> funding that actually fits what they build — in an afternoon, not a quarter.**

## Positioning statement

For **first-time and repeat deep-tech founders** who are overwhelmed by the SBIR/STTR maze, **SIGNAL**
is a **grant radar** that scores every open federal solicitation for *genuine technical fit* and
tracks every real deadline. Unlike **keyword search on SBIR.gov or generalist tools like
Instrumentl**, SIGNAL is **SBIR/STTR-native, funnel-aware, and brutally honest** — it tells you what
to skip.

## North Star Metric

**Qualified opportunities advanced** = number of tracked opportunities a user moves to "Drafting" or
beyond *after a Strong/Possible verdict*.

It captures the full value chain — discovery → trusted triage → committed action — in one number,
and it's honest: SIGNAL only wins when the founder actually pursues something worth pursuing. (Raw
"matches shown" would reward noise; "awards won" is too lagging and out of our control.)

## Strategic pillars

1. **Trust through honesty.** The verdict (why / next / flag / shot) is the product. Telling a
   founder *not* to apply is a feature. Every scoring change is judged on whether it makes the
   verdict more trustworthy.
2. **SBIR/STTR-native depth, not database breadth.** Win on funnel-awareness, agency culture,
   registration-readiness, set-asides, and awards intel — the things generalists won't build.
3. **Protect the founder's week.** Optimize for *decision speed and quality*, not time-on-site.
4. **Push, don't pull.** Discovery should arrive (digests/alerts), not require babysitting 11 sites.
5. **Right data, honestly fresh.** Ingest server-side, surface freshness, never show stale-as-live.

## What SIGNAL is NOT (anti-goals)

- ❌ A grant-writing content mill (commoditized; off-brand for a *radar*).
- ❌ A post-award compliance/reporting suite (that's Instrumentl/agency portals).
- ❌ A general grants database (nonprofit/foundation grants are out of scope).
- ❌ A consultant replacement — SIGNAL is the self-serve layer *before* (and a referrer *to*) experts.

## Business model hypothesis (for context, not a v1 commitment)

- **Free:** describe tech, see ranked matches with verdicts (the wedge / acquisition).
- **Pro (founder, ~$29–49/mo):** saved profile, deadline tracking, readiness clock, digest alerts,
  shot estimates, awards intel. Priced *far* under Instrumentl's $299, framed against a $150k–$2M
  non-dilutive prize.
- **Team/Enabler (later):** accelerators/universities/state programs — multi-company workspaces
  (Priya persona). Likely the real revenue.
- Possible: qualified referral to grant-writing consultants.

## How we'll know the strategy is working (leading signals)

- Verdict trust: % of "Strong/Possible" cards that get tracked; low rage-untracks.
- Honesty working: healthy share of "Skip/Weak" verdicts shown (we're not just flattering users).
- Activation: % of new users who complete a profile *and* track ≥1 opportunity.
- Push value: digest open/click → return-to-app rate.
